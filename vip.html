The project

A multidisciplinary team designing, prototyping, and building interactive musical hardware and software systems powered by machine learning, combining custom sensors, physical product design, and embedded systems.

My work

  • Prototyping and integrating analog accelerometers and gyroscopes with ESP32 microcontrollers, with Arduino software, to drive a custom machine-learning-based instrument.
  • Designing and prototyping a 3D physical design in SolidWorks, including mounts for the sensors and microcontrollers, so the instrument can be tracked and "taught" with machine learning.
  • Troubleshooting, debugging, and resolving all hardware and modeling issues or components of the design/prototype. This includes CAD modeling, analog circuit connections, and ergonometry of prototype.
